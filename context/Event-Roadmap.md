## MVP Complete → Launch Decision (End of Month 1)

Once MVP is polished and playable, you'll have a **vertical slice** to target an external event. Options below:

### Option A: Game Jam (Recommended First Target)
- **Event**: Ludum Dare 57 (next global game jam, ~48 hours, online)
- **Why**: Low pressure, global exposure, community feedback, credibility for portfolio
- **Fit**: Float Hero MVP is a complete, playable game — perfect jam entry
- **Timing**: Submit before submission deadline; get feedback same week
- **Post-jam**: Polish based on feedback, release on itch.io for free (or Patreon)

### Option B: Mobile App Store Soft Launch
- **Platforms**: Google Play Store (Android), Apple App Store (iOS)
- **Fit**: Idle games thrive on app stores; organic discovery + user retention
- **Prep needed**: App store screenshots, store listings, privacy policy, age rating
- **Timeline**: 2 weeks to approval after submission
- **Risk**: Approval can be slow; requires exact compliance with store guidelines

### Option C: Indonesian Game Showcase / Festival
- **Example**: Indonesia Game Awards (if applicable), or itch.io Indonesia community showcase
- **Why**: Local visibility, sponsor/publisher interest, potential funding for post-MVP features
- **Fit**: Regional idle/roguelike games are popular; cultural relevance if themed locally
- **Timeline**: Check 2026 festival dates as they're announced

### Option D: Live Service (Post-MVP)
- **Idea**: Keep MVP free on itch.io/app stores, add optional cosmetics (new hero skins, UI themes) with real-money IAP
- **Timing**: Month 2+ only (adds complexity: payment processing, analytics, balance tuning)
- **Focus shift**: Roguelike runs become daily/weekly challenges; seasonal events (fire-themed heroes, ice dungeons, etc.)

---

## Recommendation for End of Month 1
**Start with Ludum Dare or itch.io free release** — validates the game, gets community feedback, zero business pressure. Then decide whether app store submission or live-service pivot feels right based on that feedback.
