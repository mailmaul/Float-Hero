## One-liner
Mobile idle/clicker game inspired by **TBH: Task Bar Hero** with pixelated 2D art, roguelike progression elements, and passive income mechanics — built in 1 month for MVP, then expanded toward event launch next month.

## Elevator pitch
Player taps/auto-generates resources to unlock heroes, abilities, and roguelike runs. Progression feels rewarding immediately (short-term wins) but stretches toward long-term goals. Pixel art keeps it charming and fast to produce. Roguelike runs add replayability and decision-making depth later.

## Core fantasy
**"Rise from the bottom of the taskbar to hero"** — incremental power fantasy. You start weak, tap to grow stronger, passively earn while offline, then tackle harder roguelike challenges. Every 10 minutes of play feels different from the last.

## Key Pillars
- **Idle-first**: Game progresses while player is away
- **Instant gratification**: Every tap triggers visible feedback
- **Roguelike layer**: Runs are ~5-10 min, replayable, risk/reward decisions
- **Pixel aesthetic**: Simple, fast to iterate, charming
- **Mobile-native**: Thumb-friendly, landscape or portrait, single-hand play
